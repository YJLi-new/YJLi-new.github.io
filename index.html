Publications

DecoupAttn: Decoupled Sparse Attention for Training-Free Acceleration of Video Diffusion

Yanjin Li, Bingbing Qiu, Qiang Huang, Rui Qian, Jian Wang, Mingda Zhang

Semantic clusters compensate every key, and exact attention corrects their error on blocks fixed offline. Best PSNR on 9 of 10 video-generation, controlled-generation, and neural-rendering models, and up to 1.65× faster than dense attention.

First author In submission to CVPR 2027 alphaXiv

R² Flow: Recursive Self-Improvement via Recursive Skill Evolution

Mingda Zhang, Qiang Huang, Yanjin Li, Zijia Wang, Qika Lin, Xiaoying Tang, Tiesunlong Shen

Joins policy learning and skill updates in LLM agents through flow-based training and verified skill updates. State of the art on all 12 benchmarks.

Under review at ICLR 2027 arXiv:2609.33867

R²Orch: Recursive Self-Improvement for Agent Orchestration via Self-Play within a Skill-Internalization Loop

Mingda Zhang, Yun Huang, Qiang Huang, Yanjin Li, Rui Qian, Qichao Zhao, Tiesunlong Shen, Na Zhao, Jian Wang, Xiaoying Tang

Nested self-play loops let orchestration agents internalize skills, then withdraw them for zero-skill deployment.

Under review at ACL Rolling Review arXiv:2610.22740

About Me

I am a senior at UIUC. I work on efficient ML, ML systems, 3D Perception and LLM Agents.
I am applying to graduate programs for Fall 2027.

I currently work with Mingda Zhang in Prof. Xiaoying Tang's group at CUHK-Shenzhen. I am also a co-developer of ScienceClaw, an open-source agent framework for science with 1k+ GitHub stars.

My research interests include:

  • Efficient ML: cutting the compute, memory, and latency of large models without losing quality
  • ML systems: co-designing models, GPU kernels, and distributed infrastructure so that training and inference run fast on real hardware
  • 3D Perception: understanding 3D scenes from cameras and LiDAR, from detection to reconstruction and rendering
  • LLM Agents: language models that plan, use tools, and improve themselves over long multi-step tasks

Publications

Under review DecoupAttn: Decoupled Sparse Attention for Training-Free Acceleration of Video Diffusion

Yanjin Li, Bingbing Qiu, Qiang Huang, Rui Qian, Jian Wang, Mingda Zhang

TL;DR: Semantic clusters compensate every key, and exact attention corrects their error on blocks fixed offline. Best PSNR on 9 of 10 video-generation, controlled-generation, and neural-rendering models, and up to 1.65× faster than dense attention.

arXiv:2611.05917

Under review R² Flow: Recursive Self-Improvement via Recursive Skill Evolution

Mingda Zhang, Qiang Huang, Yanjin Li, Zijia Wang, Qika Lin, Xiaoying Tang, Tiesunlong Shen

TL;DR: Joins policy learning and skill updates in LLM agents through flow-based training and verified skill updates. State of the art on all 12 benchmarks.

arXiv:2609.33867

Under review R²Orch: Recursive Self-Improvement for Agent Orchestration via Self-Play within a Skill-Internalization Loop

Mingda Zhang, Yun Huang, Qiang Huang, Yanjin Li, Rui Qian, Qichao Zhao, Tiesunlong Shen, Na Zhao, Jian Wang, Xiaoying Tang

TL;DR: Nested self-play loops let orchestration agents internalize skills, then withdraw them for zero-skill deployment.

arXiv:2610.22740
